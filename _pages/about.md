
# About Me

I am Dr. Bileesh Babu, an Assistant Professor (Senior Grade 1) in the School of Computer Science and Engineering at VIT-AP University, India.

I completed my PhD in April 2025. My doctoral research focused on **Advanced Deep Learning Techniques for Automatic Target Recognition in Synthetic Aperture Radar Images**, with particular emphasis on computationally efficient deep learning architectures and synthetic SAR image generation.

My research interests include:

- Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning
- Deep Learning and Computer Vision
- Synthetic Aperture Radar Image Analysis
- Intelligent Predictive Modelling
- Applied Machine Learning for Engineering Applications

I am interested in interdisciplinary research, academic collaborations, funded research projects, and developing AI-driven solutions to real-world problems.

I am also actively involved in teaching Computer Science courses, mentoring student projects, and exploring emerging technologies in artificial intelligence.
